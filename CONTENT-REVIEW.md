# Content review

Source: corrected song(1).txt supplied 16 October 2026.

All eight songs have distinct supplied Instagram references. Source punctuation (including `।`, `॥`, `।।`, ellipsis character `…`, every period run, comma, hyphen and parenthesis) is retained exactly. Original source bytes remain unchanged.

Roman Hindi uses readable spellings such as maa, maiya, kripa, gyaan and sheronwaali. Source variants such as `मेरे` / `मेरें` are kept distinct as `mere` / `meren`. Source spellings such as `पेरो`, `पैरो`, `थोडा`, `विशवास`, `भूलु` and `भूलू` are not edited.

Structural tests are automated. Pronunciation was reviewed from the supplied text; no audio comparison to Instagram was performed. The app has no external font dependency and uses device Hindi fonts, whose appearance varies by operating system.
